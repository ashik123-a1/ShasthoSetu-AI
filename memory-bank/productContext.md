# Product Context: ShasthoSetu AI

## Why ShasthoSetu Exists
In Bangladesh, doctor-to-patient ratios are critically low in rural and semi-urban communities. Patients frequently delay care, self-medicate dangerously with over-the-counter antibiotics, or face acute shortages of emergency blood during crises.

## How ShasthoSetu Solves This
1. **Bilingual Speech & Text Interface**: Patients can speak colloquially in Bengali (Whisper-v3-turbo) or type in English/Bengali to receive guideline-grounded triage advice.
2. **Deterministic Safety Rail**: When acute red-flag symptoms are detected, the system immediately presents National Emergency Service 999 hotline instructions rather than hallucinating medical diagnosis.
3. **Blood Connect Network**: Fast 5km / 10km / 20km radius donor search with anonymized contact sharing, reducing donor discovery time from hours to seconds.
4. **Prescription Safety Gate**: Handwritten prescriptions are parsed via multi-engine OCR, with automatic rejection (`HTTP 422`) of illegible handwriting to prevent medication error.
5. **ePharma & Diagnostic Cart**: Seamless digital medicine and lab test ordering integrated with patient consultations.
