# Project Brief: ShasthoSetu AI

## Core Mission
ShasthoSetu AI (স্বাস্থ্যসেতু) is an open-source, bilingual (Bengali + English), offline-first AI healthcare intelligence platform engineered specifically for Bangladesh and underserved South Asian clinical contexts.

## Primary Objectives
1. **Bridging Healthcare Access**: Provide instant, bilingual symptom education, clinical triage, and emergency guidance (National Emergency 999 & 16263).
2. **Clinical Safety & Zero-PHI**: Ensure zero patient health information leakage using Presidio anonymization and local edge LLMs (Qwen 2.5 7B, Whisper-v3-turbo, BGE-M3).
3. **Emergency Blood Coordination**: Geo-proximity blood donor matching across all 8 administrative divisions of Bangladesh with privacy-preserving phone masking.
4. **Physician Governance & Human-in-the-Loop**: BMDC (Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council) registration verification, multi-department telemedicine queues, and strict HTTP 422 human verification gates for handwritten prescription OCR.
5. **Standardized Interoperability**: Complete Model Context Protocol (MCP) server ecosystem and multi-agent clinical coordination (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen).
